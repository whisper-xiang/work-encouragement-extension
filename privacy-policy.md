# Privacy Policy · Work Encourager / 上班鼓励器

**Last updated:** 2026-11-05

## English

Work Encourager (“the Extension”) is a Chrome browser extension that shows estimated earnings, a countdown to the end of the workday, overtime multipliers, and local charts.

### What we collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.**

The Extension has **no network / host permissions** and does not call any remote servers.

### Data stored on your device

Settings you enter (for example salary, work hours, language, currency, quotes) are saved with Chrome’s `chrome.storage.sync` API so they can sync across devices signed into the same Chrome account. That storage is managed by Google Chrome; the Extension itself does not upload this data to any third-party service.

Computed values such as today’s earnings are calculated locally in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

### Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Save and sync your settings locally via Chrome. |
| `alarms` | Refresh the toolbar badge about once per minute. |

### Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

### Contact

If you have privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository:  
https://github.com/Susie-ss/work-encouragement-extension

### Changes

We may update this policy when the Extension’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly.

## 中文

上班鼓励器（“本扩展”）是一款 Chrome 浏览器扩展，用于本地显示估算收入、下班倒计时、加班倍率与统计图表。

### 我们收集什么

**我们不收集、不传输、不出售任何个人数据。**

本扩展**没有申请网络 / 主机权限**，也不会向任何远程服务器发起请求。

### 保存在你设备上的数据

你填写的设置（如月薪、工时、语言、货币、语录等）通过 Chrome 的 `chrome.storage.sync` 保存在本地，并可随同一 Chrome 账号在多台设备间同步。该存储由 Google Chrome 管理；本扩展不会把这些数据上传到任何第三方服务。

今日已赚等金额均在浏览器本地计算，不会发送到任何地方。

### 权限说明

| 权限 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | 本地保存并同步你的设置。 |
| `alarms` | 约每分钟刷新一次工具栏徽章。 |

### 共享

我们不与第三方共享用户数据，也不使用分析、广告或追踪 SDK。
